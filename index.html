Privacy Policy

Receipto — by Being Universal • Effective: May 11, 2026

Overview

Receipto ("the App") is a receipt scanning and management application developed by Being Universal. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

Receipto does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data created within the App remains exclusively on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Receipt images captured via camera or selected from your gallery
  • Extracted receipt details (store name, amount, date, category, notes)
  • Raw OCR (optical character recognition) text from scanned receipts

This data is stored in a local database and local file storage on your device. It is never uploaded to any server.

Camera and Photo Library Access

The App requests permission to access your device camera and photo library solely for the purpose of capturing or selecting receipt images. These images are processed entirely on your device using on-device OCR technology and are never transmitted externally.

On-Device Processing

All text recognition (OCR) is performed locally on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. No images or text are sent to external servers for processing.

Third-Party Services

Receipto does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App does not contain ads.

Data Export

The App provides a CSV export feature that allows you to export your receipt data. This export is initiated entirely by you and shared through your device's standard sharing mechanism. Being Universal does not receive or have access to any exported data.

Data Deletion

You can delete any receipt at any time from within the App. Uninstalling the App will remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
